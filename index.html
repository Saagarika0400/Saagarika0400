About Me

I’m a Data Science & Applications graduate (Dec 2025) who loves turning messy, real-world data into scalable ML, AI and analytics systems. I work across the stack - from data engineering and modeling to LLM-powered insight layers - to ship solutions that people actually use.

Recently, I’ve been developing a multi-client utility-billing and financial analytics platform featuring a fully automated ETL pipeline, tariff-aware auditing workflows, and an in-house AI assistant that provides time-series forecasting, KPI explanations, and anomaly insights. Alongside this, I experiment with advanced deep-learning systems-including Vision Transformers, CNN architectures, GNMT-style translation models-and build retrieval-augmented NLP tools for more interpretable, conversational analytics.

I enjoy collaborating with engineers, analysts and business teams, explaining complex models in simple language and always tying models back to decisions: fewer manual hours, better forecasts, cleaner data, clearer stories.
