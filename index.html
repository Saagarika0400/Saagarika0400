01Spatial Omics

Spatial Transcriptomics Integration Pipeline

Co-registers Visium and Xenium spatial transcriptomics with H&E image analysis, deploying scSimilarity, CellViT, and Path2Space on HPC clusters to link tissue morphology with gene expression for spatially resolved biomarker discovery.

Foundation ModelsVisium / XeniumCellViTHPC
Problem
Tissue morphology and gene expression live in separate data types, which makes it hard to connect what cells look like with what they are doing.
Approach
Co-register Visium and Xenium data with H&E images, using scSimilarity, CellViT, and Path2Space deployed on HPC clusters.
Outcome
A pipeline that links morphology to expression for spatially resolved biomarker discovery.
